## 15.2 Regulatory findings to validate

These come from a preliminary web search on 2026-11-08. They shaped the design, so that the rules are configurable. **They are not legal advice, are not verified against primary legal text, and none is hardcoded.**

| Topic            | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Confidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Design response                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gold VAT         | A reverse charge applies to gold and diamond supplies between VAT-registered businesses; zero-rating is reported for investment-grade precious metals of 99% purity or more and for exports, which fall outside the reverse charge                      | Official statements exist ([UAE Cabinet](https://uaecabinet.ae/en/news/trading-in-gold-and-diamond-exempted-from-vat), [FTA as reported](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/vat-in-uae/fta-clarifies-vat-on-gold-diamond-deals-in-uae)). Exact conditions unverified | `taxRules` keyed on product class, purity, party registration and declared intent                                                                    |
| Jewellery VAT    | Standard 5% reported on jewellery and gold under 99%, including making charges                                                                                                                                                                          | Secondary sources only ([example](https://www.cleartax.com/ae/vat-on-gold-in-uae)). Some blogs claim a blanket exemption, which conflicts with official framing                                                                                                         | Separate tax treatment possible per value component                                                                                                  |
| E-invoicing      | Ministerial Decisions 243 and 244 of 2025 set a phased mandate over Peppol using PINT AE through accredited providers. Decision 66 of 2026 amended it. Reported dates differ across sources: large businesses around 1 Jan 2027, others around mid-2027 | Basis consistent, **dates conflict** ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/uae-framework-scope-implementation-e-invoicing-system.html), [e-invoice.app](https://www.e-invoice.app/guides/uae-e-invoicing))                                           | Invoice data captured to support structured output; `EInvoiceProvider` adapter. **This could be near-term for a large business; please check early** |
| AML reporting    | Dealers in precious metals and stones reportedly must file a DPMSR through goAML on qualifying transactions of AED 55,000 or more, plus STRs. Rules on payment mode, and intra-group treatment, conflict among sources                                  | Mostly one consultancy source ([amluae.com](https://amluae.com/aml-regulations-for-dpms-in-uae/))                                                                                                                                                                       | `complianceRules` configurable. Payment methods carry an AML class. Aggregation by party                                                             |
| Record retention | Not established by this search                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Unknown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | No TTL on any retained record; retention policy awaits advice                                                                                        |

## 15.2a Dubai market defaults (proposed, not approved)

Company settings has an **Add Dubai defaults** button. It fills in the common conventions below as a starting point, from public sources read on 2026-11-09. Most are vendor or consultancy pages, not official texts, and none replaces the sign-offs at the end of the [readiness checklist](../production-readiness.md).

| Open question    | Proposed default                                                                                              | Status                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1 Rate basis    | Fine-gram reference from spot USD/oz, 31.1034768 g per ounce, 3.6725 AED per USD, optional premium            | Purity-specific rates remain available                                                                                 |
| Q2 Fineness      | 24K 0.9999, 22K 0.9167, 21K 0.8750, 18K 0.7500                                                                | 22K is also stamped 916. Your accountant should choose                                                                 |
| Q3 Making charge | Left to each product and party. Public figures vary widely, per gram or 5 to 25%                              | Not defaulted                                                                                                          |
| Q5 Tola          | 11.6638 g, editable per company                                                                               | 11.664 is also used                                                                                                    |
| Q9 Unfixed gold  | Built: metal accounts in fine grams, with receipts, issues, price fixing and a per-party limit                | Gold loans, consignment and goldsmith job work are not modelled separately                                             |
| Q13 Currency     | AED base. Foreign invoices are converted at posting at the rate on the document; payments are recorded in AED | No exchange gain or loss on settlement, and no revaluation                                                             |
| VAT treatment    | 99% or more investment metal zero rated; registered buyers reverse charge; everything else 5%                 | Codes are created **unverified**, so they cannot be used until an adviser confirms them. Exports are not distinguished |
| AML threshold    | DPMSR at AED 55,000: individuals on cash; businesses on cash, exchange house and wires, linked over 30 days   | The 30-day window is an assumption. The regulation says "linked" without one we could verify                           |

Metal accounts carry received gold at a provisional rate until fixed, and charge VAT at the point of fixing. Both are accounting and tax judgements that need sign-off.

## 15.4 Risks

| Risk                                                            | Impact                        | Likelihood                        | Mitigation                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Calculation rules unclear or misunderstood                      | Direct financial loss         | Medium                            | Policy enums without defaults, accountant-approved vectors, stored trace                         |
| VAT or AML rules misconfigured                                  | Regulatory penalty            | Medium                            | Adviser gate; rules carry a verification status; production enablement is blocked until verified |
| E-invoicing deadline lands before the module exists             | Non-compliance                | Depends on Q25                    | Adapter planned; early decision                                                                  |
| Unfixed gold discovered late                                    | Rework of ledgers             | Medium                            | Q9 before Phase 4                                                                                |
| Transaction write conflicts at high volume on one SKU or series | Retries, latency              | Low at expected volumes           | Ordered locks; load test; series scoped per branch                                               |
| Over-scoping stalls delivery                                    | No usable product             | High for a brief this size        | Thin vertical slice validated at the end of Phase 6                                              |
| Class D documents are tamper-evident only                       | Undetected until the job runs | Low                               | `postedHash`, DB access restricted, seals                                                        |
| MongoDB suits ledgers less naturally than SQL                   | More engineering discipline   | Accepted (brief mandates MongoDB) | Replica set transactions, validators, insert-only roles, integrity jobs                          |
| Key loss makes encrypted KYC unreadable                         | Severe                        | Low                               | KMS, documented key backup and recovery                                                          |
| Dependency vulnerabilities                                      | Exposure                      | Medium                            | CI scanning, pinned versions, scheduled updates                                                  |
